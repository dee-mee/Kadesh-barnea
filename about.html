About Us

Kadesh Barnea Services Ltd.

Kadesh Barnea Services Ltd. is a dynamic company based in Nairobi, Kenya, with a unique dual focus on agribusiness and wealth management. Since our establishment in 2020, we have dedicated ourselves to building sustainable prosperity for our clients through strategic financial guidance and direct investment in the agricultural sector.

We bridge the gap between financial growth and tangible, real-world assets, offering a holistic approach to wealth creation. We empower individuals and institutions to create and sustain long-term wealth through expert financial advisory and strategic investments in high-growth agribusiness ventures.

History

Our Journey

Founded in 2020 in Nairobi, Kenya, Kadesh Barnea Services Ltd. began as a specialized financial advisory firm. Our primary goal was to provide clients with the insights needed to navigate financial markets and grow their wealth.

Recognizing the immense potential within Kenya's agricultural sector, we strategically expanded our operations to include agribusiness. This evolution allows us to offer more diverse and resilient investment opportunities, transforming our firm into a comprehensive wealth creation partner.
